# Project Principles

Every decision should reinforce these principles.

1.  Research before biography.
2.  Evidence before claims.
3.  Clarity before complexity.
4.  Editorial quality before visual effects.
5.  Timelessness before trends.
6.  Accessibility before decoration.
7.  Components before one-off solutions.
8.  Maintainability before cleverness.
9.  Build for the next ten years, not the next release.
10. When in doubt, preserve the integrity of the research program.
