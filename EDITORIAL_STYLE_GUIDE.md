# Editorial Style Guide

## Purpose

The website represents a research program, not a personal brand.

## Voice

-   Scholarly
-   Clear
-   Calm
-   Confident
-   Precise
-   International

## Show, don't advertise

Prefer:

-   examine
-   investigate
-   analyze
-   document
-   develop
-   curate
-   interpret
-   preserve
-   collaborate

Avoid:

-   transformative
-   groundbreaking
-   cutting-edge
-   world-class
-   visionary
-   disruptive
-   thought leader
-   ecosystem
-   flagship (unless factually necessary)

## Writing Rules

-   Evidence before claims.
-   Precision before persuasion.
-   Explain rather than promote.
-   Avoid slogans.
-   Avoid superlatives.
-   Keep paragraphs concise.
-   Do not invent facts or metrics.
-   First person only when it improves clarity.

The reader should conclude the work is important from the evidence---not
because the website says so.
