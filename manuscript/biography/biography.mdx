Patricia Valladares-Ruiz is Professor of Latin American and Caribbean Literature and Film at the University of Cincinnati. Her research examines how literature, film, theatre, and other cultural practices respond to political crisis, migration, and displacement in contemporary Latin America and the Caribbean. Drawing on cultural studies, memory studies, and archival theory, she explores how communities construct, preserve, and contest collective memory under conditions of rupture. Alongside her scholarship, she develops digital archives and research tools that support the documentation, preservation, and interpretation of diasporic cultural production, with a particular focus on the Venezuelan diaspora.
